Workflow

Three steps. Still offline.

Designed for focused work, not account dashboards.

  1. Install the extension

    Load AirGapKit in Chrome or Firefox. Your toolkit lives in the browser — not on a remote workspace.

  2. Drop a file into a tool

    PDF, image, spreadsheet, or text. Processing happens in memory on your device.

  3. Save the result

    Download the output when you are ready. Nothing was uploaded along the way.

Keep the gap closed.

AirGapKit is the offline file suite for people who do not want their documents touring the internet.
