# Privacy Policy for AirGapKit

**Effective Date:** July 2026

Thank you for choosing AirGapKit. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data remains yours. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use the AirGapKit Chrome Extension.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
AirGapKit is designed to be a "Privacy-First" and "Offline-First" application. **We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, files, or usage analytics.** 

## 2. Local Processing (Air-Gapped)
All operations performed by AirGapKit—including PDF manipulation, file conversions, and image editing—are processed **100% locally** on your own device within your browser. 
- Your files are **never** uploaded to any external servers or third-party cloud services.
- The extension does not require an active internet connection to function. What happens on your computer, stays on your computer.

## 3. Extension Permissions Explained
To function correctly, AirGapKit requests the following minimal browser permissions:
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to save your local UI preferences (such as your choice of Light or Dark theme). This data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us.
- **`downloads`**: Used exclusively to allow you to save and download the files you have processed (e.g., your converted PDFs or compressed images) back to your local machine.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
We do not use any third-party tracking software, analytics tools (like Google Analytics), or advertising SDKs. There is absolutely no tracking of your activity.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. However, our core philosophy of zero data collection and 100% local processing will never change.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please feel free to open an issue on our GitHub repository.
